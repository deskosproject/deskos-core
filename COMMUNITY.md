# DeskOS Community Principles

DeskOS is an open-source project built around a shared interest in Linux, open technologies, and creating a great desktop experience.

We welcome contributors from all backgrounds, regardless of their identity, beliefs, experience, or opinions. You don't need to share anyone else's worldview to participate.

We value technical merit, curiosity, honest discussion, and constructive criticism. Disagreement is a natural and necessary part of building good software. Criticizing ideas, implementations, and technical decisions is always welcome.

We expect contributors to treat each other with basic respect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Our community guidelines apply to participation in DeskOS project spaces. Contributors are free to hold and express their own views outside the project and do not speak for DeskOS unless explicitly authorized.

Maintainers are responsible for keeping discussions productive and may moderate or restrict participation when necessary. Concerns about community behavior can be raised privately with the maintainers and will be handled fairly and with discretion.

We believe good communities grow through shared work, mutual respect, and common sense rather than complicated rules.

**Everyone is welcome. Disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.**
